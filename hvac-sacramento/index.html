How Sacramento Homeowners Can Extend HVAC Life

The best way to avoid a mid-season breakdown is routine maintenance. We recommend that homeowners replace filters every one to three months, keep shrubs and grass trimmed two feet back from the outdoor condenser, rinse the condenser coil each spring, and have a professional tune-up twice a year, cooling in spring and heating in fall, so small problems get caught while they're still cheap.

Equipment age plays a big role. With regular service, a central air conditioner usually runs 12 to 15 years, a furnace 15 to 20, and a heat pump 10 to 15. Once a system passes that range and starts acting up, like rising energy bills, rooms that never reach the setpoint, or one repair after another, it's smart to start planning a replacement. A planned replacement costs less and is far less stressful than an emergency swap during the first heat wave of summer.

The earlier you report a comfort problem, the cheaper it usually is. A capacitor that's starting to fail is an easy, low-cost part to replace, yet that same capacitor, ignored through a full season, can take out the compressor and force a full system replacement. Save our number in your contacts today so you can reach us fast as soon as something seems off in your home. Early intervention saves time, money, and sleepless nights.

Typical Heating & Cooling Issues in Sacramento

HVAC systems in this area face a familiar set of problems.
